# Labs physical paper-card prototype directions

## 1. Field Sheets

**Axis:** translucent stock and imperfect hand-drawn borders. Each of the three cards is a thin colored sheet with a doubled, slightly misregistered border, visible notebook text ghosting through the surface, and handwritten notes crossing over the edge. A push-pin or tape strip provides the attachment cue. This is the closest translation of the supplied screenshot and keeps the board's route visible through the cards.

## 2. Desk Stack

**Axis:** layered paper depth and attachment order. The cards become small research slips stacked over larger sheets, with torn-looking lower edges, tape tabs, offset shadows, and a physical lift on selection. The blue route passes behind the stack and emerges from gaps, making the active card feel picked up from a desk. This direction is the most tactile but consumes more visual space.

## 3. Margin Index

**Axis:** notebook indexing and annotated interaction. Each card is pinned to a ruled page with a visible side index, hand-numbered corner, and colored pencil-like route mark. Selecting a card moves its index tab and reveals a small handwritten annotation rather than changing the whole composition. This is the clearest interaction model but the least photograph-like.

All directions use original Zxornatoe content for Packet Weather, Parrot Hours, and Open Channel. They preserve keyboard reachability, real button semantics, reduced-motion behavior, and transform/opacity-only motion.
